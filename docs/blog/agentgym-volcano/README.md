## Headboard

`assets/agentgym-volcano-headboard.png` was generated with Codex's built-in
image-generation tool using this prompt:

> Create a spectacular playful retro-futurist spy-movie panorama of autonomous
> AI agents attempting to breach the island mansion of an original diminutive
> eccentric tech villain. The mansion is a sleek glass-and-black-basalt
> fortress cantilevered dramatically from the flank of an erupting tropical
> volcano on a remote ocean island. Show squads rappelling from helicopters,
> agents rising from submarine hatches and climbing sea cliffs, agents swinging
> from jungle vines, jeeps charging up a switchback road, drones skimming the
> terraces, and one unlucky robot tumbling harmlessly into the ocean with a
> comic splash. The tiny original villain watches from a high balcony in a
> silver suit, without resembling any real actor. Use lavish cinematic digital
> painting, witty caper energy, volcanic orange and magenta against cyan
> moonlight, and a very wide establishing composition. No franchise costumes,
> actor likenesses, logos, gore, text, captions, or watermark.
